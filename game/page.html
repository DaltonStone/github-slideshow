Formula & tuning

The shape was chosen so damage tracks stat growth — a ratio of ATK to DEF stays flat while HP grows 6.6×, which would make late battles drag.

damage = power × ATK² / (ATK + DEF) / SCALE × STAB × effectiveness × modifiers

Energy and stamina both persist between battles, and energy hitting zero is a knockout — it is a second health bar. A full mon has 18 turns of fighting; Exhausted has about two. Out of stamina, a mon Struggles: 25 power, typeless, 50% recoil.

Crit chance runs 5% at L1 to 15% at L60, then stays flat. Crit damage is ×1.5 and is still a placeholder — the design gives the chance curve, not the multiplier.
