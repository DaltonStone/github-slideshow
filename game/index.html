Formula & tuning

The shape was chosen so damage tracks stat growth — a ratio of ATK to DEF stays flat while HP grows 6.6×, which would make late battles drag.

damage = power × ATK² / (ATK + DEF) / SCALE × STAB × effectiveness × modifiers
